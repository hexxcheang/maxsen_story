# Maxsen brand film: "The Name" (v6, premium)

**Format:** brand film, 60–75 seconds. Shoot in 4K so you can crop it both ways: 16:9 for the website and YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok.
**Tone:** quiet luxury. Restrained, warm, unhurried. The products and the light do most of the talking.
**Voice:** your voice, calm and close to the mic. No more than ~90 words in the whole film.
Product facts come from maxsen.sg. Anything in **[brackets]** needs a real answer from the owner.

---

## Script

### I. The father (0–12s)
- 🎥 Black. A soft click. Fade up on **older hands** (your dad's) at a workbench, soldering a small board or turning a screwdriver. Warm, low side light. Shallow focus.
- 🎥 Detail: **[an object from the original Maxsen: an old namecard, a gadget he built, a circuit board]**.
- 🎙️ "My father was an electrical engineer. He built things to solve problems."
- 🎙️ "He called his company Maxsen."

### II. The inheritance (12–22s)
- 🎥 The same bench, now **your hands**, younger, setting down the old object and picking up a Lusano+ Prestige faceplate.
- 🎙️ "I studied engineering too. And I kept his name."
- 🎙️ "But I wanted to build something different. Something beautiful."

### III. The object (22–38s)
Slow macro shots, one idea per shot. Let each one breathe.
- 🎥 **Lusano+ Prestige:** a downlight slowly moves across the face, and the Swarovski crystals catch it and scatter.
- 🎥 **Halo:** a fingertip turns the knurled metal ring. The mother-of-pearl face shimmers as it turns.
- 🎥 **Lusano Noble:** a slow slide along Italian Fenix laminate to the thin line of crystal at the edge.
- 🎥 **Nova+ Pro:** in a dark room, the name on a single key glows softly: *Evening*.
- 🎙️ "The things you touch every day should be worth touching."
- 📝 Small, elegant on-screen text over the crystal shot: *Genuine Swarovski crystal*

### IV. The intelligence (38–50s)
- 🎥 A hand presses *Evening*. Across a real home, the cove, downlights and curtains all move together in one slow, continuous take.
- 🎙️ "And intelligence you never have to think about."
- 🎥 A long corridor to the furthest room. A touch, and the room answers instantly.
- 🎙️ "Every switch speaks to the next, room to room. One home, one gateway."

### V. The light (50–60s)
- 🎥 A dining table at dusk. The light slides from crisp daylight down to deep, amber candlelight. Faces and timber go warm.
- 🎙️ "And now, light itself. From daylight to candlelight."

### VI. The return (60–72s)
- 🎥 Your dad walks into the finished room. He reaches out and touches the switch. The room settles into warm light.
- 🎥 He looks at it for a moment. **[If he says something real and short, keep it, with subtitles. If not, stay silent and let the look carry it.]**
- 🎙️ "The name was his. What it becomes is mine."
- 🎥 Fade to black.
- 📝 End card: **MAXSEN** / *A beautiful home deserves beautiful intelligence.* / maxsen.sg

## Production notes
- **Light:** warm and low, with deep shadows. Avoid flat, bright showroom lighting. Shoot the crystal with a single moving light source so it sparkles.
- **Sound:** sound matters as much as the picture. Record the real click of each switch, the turn of the Halo ring and the curtain track up close. Under it, sparse piano or strings with no beat. Leave a moment of near-silence before the final line.
- **Colour grade:** warm blacks, soft highlights, gentle contrast. No saturated colours.
- **Pacing:** shots of 2–4 seconds, no jump cuts, no zooms, no trending sounds, no emoji on screen.
- **Text:** at most 3 on-screen text moments, in a thin serif or light sans-serif that matches the website.
- **Locations:** the Tampines showroom for the product macros, and one real finished client home (with permission) for IV–VI.
- **1,800 K shot:** use a Light Alchemist fitting. Standard Maxsen downlights stop at 2,700 K.
- **Dad:** film him before he knows what to expect, with the camera already rolling. If he isn't comfortable on camera, section I can be his hands only and section VI can be just his hand on the switch.
