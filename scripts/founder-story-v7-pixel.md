# Maxsen brand film: "The Name" (v7, pixel art, faceless)

Adapted from the premium v6 for **Higgsfield** in a pixel-art style. **No real person appears.** Father and son are pixel
figures, shown only as hands, silhouettes or from behind, with no faces.

**Format:** 6 blocks × 10 seconds = 60s, plus a 4s end card added in editing.
**Each block:** one narration line + 5 shots of about 2 seconds each (this matches how Higgsfield's narrated video workflow builds clips).
**Aspect:** 9:16 for Reels/TikTok. Generate a 16:9 version separately for the website or YouTube if needed.

---

## Visual style lock (use for every block)
> Cinematic 16-bit pixel art, like a modern premium indie game. Warm, restrained palette: deep navy shadows, amber and
> soft gold light, muted ivory walls. Rich lighting with soft glow and bloom, crisp pixel edges, slow gentle camera moves.
> Quiet, elegant, nostalgic mood. No faces visible on any person: hands, silhouettes, or figures from behind only.
> No text, no logos, no UI.

**Why pixel art can still feel premium:** keep the palette tight and warm, keep the moves slow, and use a soft piano score
instead of chiptune music. It should feel like a memory, not a game.

**Rule for the prompts:** don't put real brand names (Maxsen, Swarovski) into the generation prompts. Describe the look
instead, and add the names afterwards as text in editing.

## End card (60–64s), added in editing, not generated
The last pixel frame **dissolves into a real photo** of Lusano+ Prestige (from the website's `assets/`), then:
**MAXSEN** / *A beautiful home deserves beautiful intelligence.* / maxsen.sg

The move from pixels to a real photo shows that the story ends in a real product. The end card is also where the exact
product is shown accurately, because AI-generated pixel switches won't match your real designs.

**Voice:** calm, warm, unhurried male voice. Use your own recording, or a Higgsfield AI voice if you'd rather not be heard
either. **Music:** sparse, soft piano. No chiptune.

## Before generating
1. **Narrator voice:** your own recording, or an AI voice?
2. **Subtitles:** burned in (recommended for social, since most people watch muted), or none?
3. **End card photo:** confirm which real product photo to use (Lusano+ Prestige is the default).
