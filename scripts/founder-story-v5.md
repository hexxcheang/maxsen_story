# Maxsen founder video: "Beautiful + Smart" (v5)

Format: vertical video for TikTok / Instagram Reels, 45–55 seconds.
Product facts come from maxsen.sg (site sources in `hexxcheang/maxsen_website`).
Anything in **[brackets]** still needs a real answer from the owner.

---

## Script

### 1. Hook (0–3s)
- 🎥 Slow pan across a plain white plastic switch on a typical Singapore wall.
- 📝 On screen: **"You touch this every day. When did you last look at it?"**

### 2. The belief (3–12s)
- 🎥 You, at the showroom or a workbench.
- 🎙️ "Switches and lights are what you see and touch most at home. Most of them are designed to be ignored."
- 🎙️ "I graduated in mechanical engineering from NUS. My friends took jobs. I took my dad's company name, Maxsen, and started building the switches I wished existed."

### 3. Reveal: beautiful (12–26s)
- 🎥 Hard cut: the same wall, now with a **Lusano+ Prestige**. The crystals catch the downlight.
- 🎙️ "Genuine Swarovski crystal. We're a certified Swarovski Branding Partner."
- 🎥 Quick close-ups, one beat each:
  - **Halo**: a hand turns the knurled metal ring and the room dims. Turns the mother-of-pearl face and the light warms.
  - **Lusano Noble**: Italian Fenix laminate edged with a thin line of crystal.
  - **Nova+ Pro**: every key lit with its name ("Evening", "Movie", "Night").
- 🎙️ "Hardware in your home should be beautiful."

### 4. Reveal: smart (26–40s)
- 🎥 One press on an "Evening" key. Downlights, cove and curtains all respond together.
- 🎙️ "And actually smart. Most smart homes are smart until the Wi-Fi drops."
- 🎥 Walk to the furthest room of an HDB flat and tap. It responds instantly.
- 🎙️ "Our switches pass the signal to each other, room to room, so concrete walls don't break the connection. Everything runs through one Maxsen gateway."
- 🎥 Light slides from cool daylight down to amber candlelight.
- 🎙️ "And now, lights. From 6,000 K daylight down to 1,800 K candlelight, from the same downlight."

### 5. Proof (40–47s)
- 🎥 Fast montage of real installs: a BTO flat, a condo, a landed house.
- 🎙️ "Four years in, we've done 400+ homes across Singapore." *(see check #1)*

### 6. Ending (47–55s)
- 🎥 Dad presses one of your switches. **[His real reaction / line]**
  - *If Dad isn't on camera:* wide shot of a finished living room as the lights come on.
- 🎙️ "The name was my dad's. Making it beautiful *and* smart is mine."
- 📝 On screen: **"Maxsen. A beautiful home deserves beautiful intelligence."**

**Caption:** "Be honest: what do the switches in your home look like? 👇 Come try them at our Tampines showroom."

## Checks before filming
1. **400+ homes:** the site lists 200+ / 100+ / 100+, which adds up to 400+. Confirm you're happy to say "400+", or use "hundreds of homes".
2. **1,800 K is the Light Alchemist upgrade**, not standard. If you film it, use an upgraded fitting.
3. **Dad's line.** Still the one detail that isn't on the website. Is he willing to be on camera?
4. **The website has no founder story.** Once the video is out, consider adding a short "Our story" section so the two match.
